Privacy & cookies

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Bozhenko Digital is a small video production and digital marketing studio in Limerick, Ireland. We are responsible (the “controller”) for the personal data you send us. You can reach us at bozhenkodigital@gmail.com or on WhatsApp +353 87 371 4022.

What we collect

Only what you type into our order and quote forms: your WhatsApp number, email, name (optional), your business website or social page, and the details of the work you need. If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we also receive what you send there. We don’t run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

To reply to your request, agree the details, make and deliver your video, and invoice you. This is processing needed to take steps at your request before a contract and to carry it out (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We keep invoices and order records because Irish tax law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)). We never sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who else handles it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our email inbox.
  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps short technical server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), only if you choose to message us there.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on the safeguards GDPR allows, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to an order are deleted within 12 months. Records of orders and invoices are kept for 6 years, as Irish Revenue rules require, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, or receive a copy of it, by emailing bozhenkodigital@gmail.com. We’ll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and our fonts are hosted on our own server, so no data goes to Google Fonts. Your browser keeps these small notes, only on your device:
