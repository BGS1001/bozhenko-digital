Privacy

Bozhenko Digital (Limerick, Ireland) collects only the details you enter in the order form: WhatsApp number, email, business website or social account, and information about the video you need.

Why: to reply to your request, agree the details and deliver your video. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long: we keep order details for as long as needed to provide the service and meet accounting obligations, then delete them.

Processors: form submissions are delivered to our email inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).

Your rights: under GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting us.

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies.
