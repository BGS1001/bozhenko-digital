Privacy & cookies

Bozhenko Digital (Limerick, Ireland) collects only the details you enter in our order and quote forms: WhatsApp number, email, name (optional), business website or social account, and information about the work you need.

Why: to reply to your request, agree the details and deliver your video. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long: we keep order details for as long as needed to provide the service and meet accounting obligations, then delete them.

Processors: form submissions are delivered to our email inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).

Your rights: under GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting us.

Cookies: this site uses only essential storage. Your browser keeps a small note of your cookie choice so the notice doesn't show again. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the site.

Third parties: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
