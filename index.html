Quick answers

Do you come out to film or take photos?

No, and you won’t need us to. We work with content you already have: customer video reviews, photos of your work, product shots and clips from your website or social pages.

What do I need to send?

A link to your website or social page is usually enough. If you have extra photos or videos, send them along too, and let us know which offer you’d like to highlight.

What do you mean by current ad formats?

Ad structures widely used on Reels and TikTok at the moment, such as a strong opening few seconds, customer reactions, before-and-after reveals and quick-cut showcases. We choose the one that suits your business and adapt it to your content.

How fast will I get my video?

Usually within 24–72 hours once we have your content. Packs are delivered in batches, so you can start posting straight away.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories as standard. 4:5 and 1:1 for feed ads on request.

What's in the 4 and 8 Reels packs?

Separate ads for your key dates and sales, for example Black Friday, Christmas, the January sales, Valentine’s Day or your own promotions. You choose the occasions.

Can you also run the ads for me?

Yes, if that would help. See Growth support below: campaign setup and management are available as an optional extra.
