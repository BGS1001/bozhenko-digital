Scene 07A few straight answers

Before you order.

Do I need to film anything?

No. Our standard ads are made from what you already have: video reviews, photos of your work and clips from your website or social pages. If you’d like fresh footage, a video or photo shoot is available as an extra, priced on request.

When do I pay?

Not until you’ve received your finished draft. We make your ad first and send it to you, and only then ask for payment.

What if I haven’t much content?

A few good photos and a short clip are usually plenty. If we need more, we’ll let you know.

How fast will I get my video?

Usually within 24–72 hours once we have your link. Packs are delivered in batches, so you can start posting straight away.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories as standard. 4:5 and 1:1 for feed ads on request.

What’s in the 4 and 8 Reels packs?

Separate ads for your key dates and sales, for example Black Friday, Christmas, the January sales, Paddy’s Day or your own promotions. You choose the occasions.

Can you run the ads for me as well?

We can, if that would help: Meta, Google and TikTok campaigns, set up and looked after. See Extras and request a quote.
