Quick answers

What do I need to send?

Whatever you have: photos, short clips, logo, prices and the offer you want to promote. If something's missing we'll tell you what would help.

How fast will I get my video?

Usually within 24–72 hours once we have your materials. Packs are delivered in batches so you can start posting straight away.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories as standard. 4:5 and 1:1 for feed ads on request.

What's in the 4 and 8 Reels packs?

Separate ads for your key dates and sales — for example Black Friday, Christmas, New Year sale, Valentine's Day or your own promotions. You choose the occasions.

Can you also run the ads for me?

Yes, if you want. See Growth support below — campaign setup and management are available as an add-on.
